More About Me

I'm a Software Engineering Technology student at Universitas Gadjah Mada. I live in the terminal, configure things that don't need configuring, and have strong opinions about text editors.

Outside of that, I watch a lot of films, mostly Wong Kar-Wai and anything that lingers. I listen to albums front-to-back, read whatever I can get my hands on, from Descartes to Dostoevsky to Tan Malaka, and I'm slowly trying to build a working understanding of law, history, finance, and philosophy alongside the engineering.

The cinema thing isn't just an aesthetic preference. It shapes how I think about software: deliberate structure, intentional flow, nothing without a reason.
